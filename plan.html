Name: Shehzad Rashid Chowdhury

Goal

Develop a clear, accessible and mobile-friendly small website for 100SMILES Dental Care that increases patient bookings and awareness of routine and preventative dental services.

Success Evaluation

  • Number of appointment requests originating from the website from submission of form.
  • Nummber of telephone enquiries from pages that contain the contact number
  • User feedback collection
  • Time on page and page visits for key pages (Home, Services) for increased engagement.

Target Audience

Primary audience: Local families in Razole and surrounding suburbs seeking reliable dental care for all ages.
Design considerations: To suit the audience, the webiste will be simple, easy to navigate, mobile mobile-friendly and will use colors (white, light blue and dark blue). Prominent contact details, opening hours and booking appointment via calls and email will be displayed.

Flowchart

The website will be straightforward with the homepage linking to three main sections:

Pages: Home → Services → Staff (About Us) → Contact Us
